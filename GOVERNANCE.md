# Governance

Flare is a young project. This document describes how it is run today, and it will change as more
people get involved.

## Roles

- **Contributors** are everyone who opens issues, reviews changes or sends pull requests.
- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, triage issues, publish releases and enforce the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- The **lead maintainer** has the final say when maintainers can't reach consensus, and looks after the
  project's name and logo ([TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)), the official app-store accounts and the
  official push relay.

## How decisions are made

- **Day-to-day changes** are decided in pull requests. One maintainer's approval is enough to merge.
  While there is only one maintainer, they may merge their own changes.
- **Bigger changes** (new components, breaking API or configuration changes, changes to this document
  or to the trademark policy) start as a proposal issue. It stays open for comments for at least
  7 days, and is accepted by consensus among the maintainers. If there is no consensus, the lead
  maintainer decides.
- The project's code stays under the Apache License 2.0.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a track record of solid contributions and reviews may be invited to become
maintainers by consensus of the existing maintainers. Maintainers who have been inactive for six months
may be moved to emeritus status, and can return by asking.
